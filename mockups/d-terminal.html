Amazon Web Services Guide

cloud/aws · intermediate · 50m · updated 2026

mental-model

AWS is organized as regions, availability zones, and edge locations. Think in failure domains first — design for zonal isolation, then regional recovery. Control plane and data plane have different reliability contracts.

Most production systems span at least two AZs. Multi-region is a deliberate cost trade for true DR. Prefer describe-regions style mental models over marketing names.

iam

Everything is a principal acting on a resource under a policy. Prefer roles over long-lived keys. Scope permissions to the smallest action set that still works. Deny evaluates first.

compute

EC2 is the substrate. EKS/ECS orchestrate containers; Fargate removes nodes. Lambda for event-driven work with hard duration limits.
